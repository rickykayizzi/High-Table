Please note: Hightable is a developing website. Some features described in this policy may change as new services are introduced. We aim to update this page when our information practices change.

1. Who we are

Hightable is a Kampala-first events, lifestyle, media and discovery platform. Our aim is to help visitors discover events, venues, experiences, stories and accommodation.

This policy applies to your use of hightable.site and information you voluntarily send to us through the contact methods we provide.

Hightable is intended to serve visitors in Uganda and elsewhere. We will identify the appropriate business or data controller details here as the organisation's formal contact arrangements are finalised.

2. Information we may receive

The information involved depends on how you use the website. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

  • Information you provide: for example, your name, email address, organisation, event details and message when you contact us.
  • Technical information: the website hosting provider or other infrastructure services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times and server logs.
  • Information from external services: if we introduce analytics, embedded media or other integrations, those providers may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, identification documents or other sensitive information through general contact channels unless we have specifically established a secure and appropriate process.

3. Contact enquiries

The contact page currently prepares an email using the visitor's email application. The website itself does not send that email or store the form contents in a Hightable database.

If you choose to send the prepared email, the information will be processed by your email provider and the recipient's email provider. Once received, Hightable may use your message to respond to your enquiry, assess a listing, discuss coverage or consider a business partnership.

If you contact us through WhatsApp or another external service, that service's own terms and privacy practices also apply.

Please be aware that opening an email draft does not itself transmit your information. You decide whether to send it.

4. Cookies and analytics

The current website is primarily a static website. Its interactive directory pages use browser-side code to display and filter the information included in those pages.

We have not represented that Hightable currently uses advertising cookies, behavioural tracking or an analytics platform. If we add analytics, advertising technology or other tools that use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

The hosting provider or services used to deliver the website may process technical request data as part of their normal operations.

5. Sharing information

We do not intend to sell personal contact information to third parties.

Information may be handled by service providers when necessary to operate the website or respond to enquiries. If information is shared with a service provider, we aim to limit that sharing to what is appropriate for the relevant purpose.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users or services.

If Hightable introduces advertising, sponsored listings, promotional partnerships or other commercial services, we will distinguish commercial content appropriately and update this policy if the way information is handled changes.

6. Third-party links and embedded content

Hightable may link to event ticketing pages, venue websites, accommodation providers, social media platforms, video services and other external websites.

These services are operated independently. We do not control their privacy practices, security or content. Review their policies before providing personal information or making a purchase.

A link to a business or event does not, by itself, mean that Hightable endorses or guarantees that provider.

7. Data retention and security

Information sent to Hightable by email or another contact service may remain in those systems for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant business records or meet legal obligations.

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. However, no website, transmission method or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because the website's features may evolve, retention and security arrangements may change when new services are added.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can choose whether to send an enquiry through the contact page. You can also choose not to provide optional information. Some information may be needed if you want us to respond to your request.

To make a privacy request, use the contact page and clearly explain your request. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Legal requirements and legitimate record keeping may affect what we can delete or disclose.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable requirements, including relevant Ugandan data protection law where it applies.

9. Children's privacy

Hightable is a general audience discovery and lifestyle platform and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Hightable inappropriately, please contact us so we can assess the matter and take appropriate steps where required.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the website, its services or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page. The “Last updated” date at the top should be changed whenever we make a substantive revision.

11. Contact us about privacy

If you have a question about this policy or wish to make a privacy request, please contact Hightable through our Contact page.
