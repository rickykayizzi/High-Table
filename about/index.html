What is Hightable?

Hightable is a Kampala-first discovery and lifestyle platform designed to bring more of the city's experiences into one convenient place.

Finding something to do should not mean searching through endless posts, scattered event announcements and disconnected recommendations. Hightable aims to make discovering your next plan easier.

From brunches and concerts to new hangout spots, weekend getaways and stories about the people shaping local culture, the goal is to help people find experiences that fit their interests.

✦

More discovery. More connection. More living.

A growing home for local experiences, useful guides, community stories and the places people want to talk about.

Why we're building it

Kampala has events, creative people, interesting places and experiences worth sharing. Hightable aims to make them easier to discover.
